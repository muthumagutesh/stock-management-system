# APEXSTOCK™ INDIA EDITION - GST COMPLIANCE & TAXATION GUIDE
**Statutory Guide to Indian Goods & Services Tax (GST), HSN Codes, Invoicing, and e-Way Bills**

## 1. Statutory Framework

**APEXSTOCK™ India Edition** is engineered in full compliance with the statutory regulations laid down by the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) under:
- **Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (CGST Act)**
- **Integrated Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (IGST Act)**
- **State Goods and Services Tax Acts (SGST Acts)**
- **National e-Way Bill System Rules**

## 2. Tax Determination: Intra-State vs. Inter-State

The system automatically identifies whether an order is **Intra-State** or **Inter-State** based on the first two digits of the Supplier's and Customer's **GSTIN** (State Code):

```
                        +----------------------------+
                        |  Supplier GSTIN: 27...     |  (State: Maharashtra)
                        +--------------+-------------+
                                       |
                                       v
               +-----------------------------------------------+
               | Check Customer/Receiver GSTIN State Prefix    |
               +-----------------------+-----------------------+
                                       |
                   +-------------------+-------------------+
                   |                                       |
          Same State (Prefix 27)                 Different State (!= 27)
                   |                                       |
                   v                                       v
       +-----------------------+               +-----------------------+
       |   INTRA-STATE SUPPLY  |               |  INTER-STATE SUPPLY   |
       |  CGST: Half of GST %  |               |  IGST: Full GST %     |
       |  SGST: Half of GST %  |               |  (e.g., 18% IGST)     |
       |  (e.g., 9% + 9%)      |               |                       |
       +-----------------------+               +-----------------------+
```

### 2.1 Formulae Implemented in Backend & Frontend
For an item with Taxable Value $V$ and GST Rate $R\%$:

1. **Intra-State Supply (Both entities in Maharashtra - Code 27)**:
   $$\text{CGST Amount} = \frac{V \times (R / 2)}{100}$$
   $$\text{SGST Amount} = \frac{V \times (R / 2)}{100}$$
   $$\text{IGST Amount} = 0$$
   $$\text{Total Invoice Amount} = V + \text{CGST Amount} + \text{SGST Amount}$$

2. **Inter-State Supply (Supplier in 27, Customer in 29 Karnataka, 20 Jharkhand, etc.)**:
   $$\text{CGST Amount} = 0$$
   $$\text{SGST Amount} = 0$$
   $$\text{IGST Amount} = \frac{V \times R}{100}$$
   $$\text{Total Invoice Amount} = V + \text{IGST Amount}$$

## 4. Statutory Requirements for Tax Invoices (Section 31)

Under Rule 46 of the CGST Rules, 2017, every B2B tax invoice generated by ApexStock incorporates:
1. **Invoice Title**: Prominently stated as `TAX INVOICE`.
2. **Identification**: Unique sequential invoice number not exceeding 16 characters (e.g., `SO-IN-2026-0001`).
3. **Date of Issue**: Statutory date in `DD-MM-YYYY` format.
4. **Supplier Particulars**: Name, address, and GSTIN (`27AAACA9999P1Z8`).
5. **Recipient Particulars**: Name, address, GSTIN/UIN, and Place of Supply.
6. **HSN / SAC Code**: Mapped for each line item.
7. **Description of Goods**: Technical specifications, batch lot, and physical unit of quantity.
8. **Value of Goods**: Taxable value, trade discount applied, and net taxable turnover.
9. **Rate of Tax**: Distinct columns for CGST %, SGST %, or IGST %.
10. **Amount of Tax**: Exact tax charged broken down into Central, State, and Integrated Tax.
11. **Place of Supply**: State and 2-digit State code.
12. **Signatory**: Authorized digital signature placeholder.

## 5. Electronic Way Bill (e-Way Bill) Automation

### 5.1 Mandate & Threshold
Under Rule 138 of the CGST Rules, an **e-Way Bill** is mandatory for any consignment of goods where the total consignment value exceeds **₹50,000**.

### 5.2 System Integration in ApexStock
- **Automatic Flagging**: Whenever a Sales Order or Purchase Order exceeds ₹50,000 in total value, the system automatically marks the order as requiring an e-Way bill.
- **e-Way Bill Number Field**: Operators record the 12-digit Government NIC e-Way Bill number (e.g., `291049281734`) upon dispatch.
- **Tracking & Validation**: Displayed prominently on warehouse dispatch manifests and customer tax invoices to ensure smooth passage through interstate tax checkposts without detention.

## 6. Input Tax Credit (ITC) Reconciliation

ApexStock records all supplier invoices with individual CGST/SGST/IGST breakdown to streamline monthly **GSTR-2B / GSTR-3B** filing:
- All purchase order tax amounts are accumulated under `taxable_amount`, `cgst_amount`, `sgst_amount`, and `igst_amount`.
- Exportable via the **Analytics & Reports** module directly into Excel or CSV for automated upload into the GSTN offline tool or third-party GST filing software (ClearTax, Computex, Tally).
